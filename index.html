Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How early should we book our event with Meetco?

We recommend booking weddings and large corporate summits at least 3 to 6 months in advance to secure top-tier venues and preferred dates. For birthday parties and photography sessions, 2 to 4 weeks is typically sufficient.

Can I customize the services in my booking?

Yes! All our packages can be customized. You can select specific services such as photography only, decor only, or full-service turnkey event coordination using our Booking page.

What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?

We offer flexible rescheduling options up to 30 days prior to your event date without penalty fees. Detailed terms are provided in your personalized event contract upon booking.

Do you manage destination events outside Singapore?

Yes, our team operates worldwide! We have coordinated luxury destination weddings and international summits in Bali, Dubai, Paris, New York, and Melbourne.
